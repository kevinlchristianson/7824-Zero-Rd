Sources & assumptions

  • Plan is scaled from Google Earth. The ruler reads 82.33 ft along the north leg's north roof edge, which is about 0.33 ft per pixel. Overall proportions were checked against the listing's overhead drone shot.
  • The north leg's main block has the center's footprint turned 90°. The extra length to the measured roof edge is a vestibule behind the four west arches.
  • The center wall stack is 11′ ground level, then 2′ of floor structure, then 9′ upper level, for a 22′ plate.
  • Leg wall heights, the 5:12 roof pitch, the 1′‑6″ overhang, and door and window placement are estimates from the listing photos.
  • All inputs live in model/building.js. npm run export writes OBJ and GLB files to export/.
